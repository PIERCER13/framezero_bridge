## EN
**One API for every FiveM framework. Free dependency of all FrameZero products.**

Requires: framezero_bridge (free, included). Works on ESX, QBCore, Qbox, vRP, vRPex and Creative.

- ESX, QBCore, Qbox, vRP, vRPex, Creative and standalone
- Auto-detects inventory, garages, notifications, target and more
- `custom/` folder for overrides
